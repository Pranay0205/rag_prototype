EUR/USD implied volatility is at an 18-month high heading into 2027.

US corporates with growing euro cost bases are lifting hedge ratios from about 25 percent to between 50 and 75 percent of forecast exposure.

We recommend layered 12-month forward programs for clients with new, predictable euro costs, for example after European acquisitions.
