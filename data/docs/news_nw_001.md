Columbus, Ohio. Northwind Logistics Inc. said on Tuesday it has agreed to acquire Rotterdam-based Van Dijk Opslag for EUR 310 million, its first European acquisition.

The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approval. Chief executive Tom Becker said the purchase gives Northwind a warehousing footprint serving its largest European customers.

Northwind said it would fund the purchase with cash on hand and new debt.
